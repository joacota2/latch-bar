# Latch Bar name and branding

Latch Bar's source code is licensed under Apache 2.0. That license permits commercial use and forks; these guidelines do not change those permissions.

The official project is [joacota2/latch-bar](https://github.com/joacota2/latch-bar). Builds published through that repository's Releases page are the project's official builds.

## Describing your fork

- Use a distinct product name and icon for modified distributions so users can tell them apart from official Latch Bar builds.
- Clearly identify your distribution as an independent fork and name its maintainer. A description such as “Based on Latch Bar” with a link to the original repository is welcome.
- Do not imply that the Latch Bar maintainer endorses, signs, supports, or publishes your build.
- Preserve the copyright, license, and attribution notices required by the applicable licenses. A distinct product name does not remove those obligations.

Accurate references to Latch Bar in reviews, comparisons, documentation, and statements of compatibility are welcome. Apache 2.0 Section 6 does not grant general trademark rights; its attribution exceptions and any rights granted by applicable law still apply. This policy does not assert that a trademark is registered, prohibit lawful descriptive use, or add a restriction on commercial use of the code.

For proposed use of the name or logo as your own product branding, contact the maintainer through the [project's GitHub issues](https://github.com/joacota2/latch-bar/issues) before using it.
